Returns & 30-day trial

Fountains and feeders come with a 30-day trial. Use it with your pet; if they won't switch, send it back for a refund.

The 30-day trial

Buy a fountain or feeder, set it up, and let your pet decide over thirty days.

If they won't switch, send it back for a full refund of the product price. No long questionnaire, no restocking fee.

How to return

Write to us and we'll send a return label or instructions.

The buyer pays return shipping unless the unit arrived faulty. If you're in the EU or UK, you also keep your statutory 14-day withdrawal right.

Consumables

Filters and pumps can't be returned once opened, except if faulty.

If a filter or pump fails, we'll replace it.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method once we receive the item.

They usually land within five to ten business days.
