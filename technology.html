Quiet is a spec

We treat noise, cleaning and materials as design targets, not afterthoughts. Here is how the numbers work.

Quiet, measured

A running fountain should be background, not a soundtrack. We tune the pump and mount it so the whole unit sits near 24 decibels at one metre — roughly a quiet library, soft enough to forget beside a bed or a desk. The Spring 01 runs the same standard across a 2.8-liter bowl, with the pump isolated to keep vibration off the counter.

Built to come apart

Cleaning should feel like washing up, not servicing a machine. Every fountain breaks into five parts in about thirty seconds, with no tools, and every part that touches water is dishwasher-safe. The filter swaps out in moments and the pump slides free when it is time to replace it. That is the whole routine — take it apart, wash it, put it back.

Materials that touch water

Everything a pet drinks from is either 316 stainless steel or glazed ceramic — surfaces that stay neutral, resist scratches and take a proper clean. We avoid plastics wherever water sits. The result is a fountain that does not stain, smell or hold onto the previous week, and that stays looking new far longer than the price suggests.
