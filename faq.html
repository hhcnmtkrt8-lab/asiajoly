FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

The things people actually ask, before and after they bring something home.

For two cats, the Spring 01 is the one we suggest. Its 2.8-liter bowl holds enough water that two cats can drink freely between refills, and the wide surface gives both room to approach without crowding. If space is tight, the Spring 02 at 2.0 liters works well, just with a smaller footprint.

Quiet is one of the first things we design for. The pump is tuned and mounted to sit around 24 decibels at one metre, which is about the level of a very quiet library. In a bedroom, you are more likely to hear the water than the motor, and most test homes said they stopped noticing it within a day or two.

For a fountain, rinse and refill the water every few days and give the full five parts a proper wash about once a week. The whole thing comes apart in thirty seconds with no tools, so a weekly clean takes less time than making coffee. The filter swaps every thirty days, and the pump is replaceable when it eventually wears.

Yes, and you should expect to. The pump is a part, not the product, and it is rated for about eighteen months. When it wears out, you swap in a new one for $18 instead of replacing the whole fountain. It slides out in seconds, so a worn pump never means a dead product.

All parts that touch water or food are dishwasher-safe — the bowl, the tray and everything that comes apart. On fountains that is all five parts, and on the bowls and mats the stoneware, stainless and silicone can all go through. Pop them on the top rack and skip the heated dry if you want to be gentle.

Many cats take to moving water quickly, and cats often drink more when water is moving rather than sitting still. That said, cats are cats. Give yours a few days, keep the fountain near a spot they already like, and run it continuously at first so they get used to the sound. Many hesitate less than we expect.

Yes, and the lower, wider bowls are the reason. A small dog can drink from any of our fountains comfortably; the still bowls and mats work for dogs and cats alike. If you have a larger dog, the Spring 01 and its 2.8-liter bowl give the most water and the easiest reach.

Both share the same bowl, pump and quiet standard. The Pro adds two things: an app for intake tracking and low-water alerts, and a 72-hour battery backup so the fountain keeps running through a power cut. If you want the fountain and nothing more, the Spring 01 is enough. If you want the data and the backup, take the Pro.

Filters come in a three-pack for $19, and each filter lasts about thirty days. If you subscribe, we send a three-pack on a schedule that matches your fountain — roughly one pack every three months — and you can pause, skip or cancel anytime. No lock-in, and the price stays the same whether you subscribe or buy as you go.

Every product carries a warranty against manufacturing defects, and our support team is the place to start if anything arrives wrong or stops working early. Because the pump and filter are replaceable, most problems are solved with a spare part rather than a return. Check the product page for the exact terms for your region.

Give it a little time first — a week is usually enough for a cat or a small dog to come around. If yours genuinely does not take to it, reach out to support and we will arrange a return under our trial terms. We would rather the object go to a home where it gets used.
