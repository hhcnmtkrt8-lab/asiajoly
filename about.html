About

A small team, an honest product

Asiajoly is a small team designing considered objects for cats and dogs, made with partners in Shenzhen and Chaozhou.

Why we started

We began in 2024 with a simple irritation. Most pet products fell into two camps: clinical white plastic that looked like medical equipment, or bright cartoonish toys that felt aimed at children. Neither belonged in a home where the kettle, the lamp and the coffee cup were chosen with care. We believed a cat or a dog deserved the same thought. So we started building objects we would want on our own counters.

Made with real partners

The metal and electronics come from partners in Shenzhen, a city that knows precision manufacturing. The ceramics come from Chaozhou, where stoneware has been shaped for centuries. We do not own the factories and we do not pretend to. We work directly with them, visit them, and push on the details that matter to us: tolerances, glazes, quiet pumps, and parts you can actually replace.

Designed for small apartments

Most of our customers rent, and most live in a city where space is the real luxury. So we design from the footprint up: fountains that sit beside a kettle, feeders that do not crowd a counter, bowls that slide under a shelf. Quiet matters because the bedroom and the living room are often the same room. Clean matters because there is nowhere to hide a dirty thing.

What we make, and keep

We make four lines — Spring fountains, Feast feeders, Still bowls and mats, Play toys — plus Care, the filters and pumps that keep them running. We sell fewer things on purpose, and we design each one to be taken apart, washed and repaired rather than thrown away. The goal is not a full catalog. It is a small set of objects that earn their place for years.

— June, Founder

What we hold to

01

Honesty over hype

We state specs plainly, admit what a toy cannot survive, and refuse to invent awards, reviews or urgency just to push a sale.

02

Design that disappears

Good pet objects should blend into a countertop, run quietly, and stay clean with little effort, so the pet never overwhelms the home.

03

Repair over replace

The filter and the pump are meant to be swapped, not the whole machine. We would rather sell you a part than another product.
