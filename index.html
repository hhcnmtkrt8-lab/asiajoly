01 — Why we exist

Pet things that look like your home

Most pet products come in two flavors: white plastic that looks like a clinic, or loud colors that look like a toy aisle. We wanted neither. We started Asiajoly because the things a cat or dog needs every day should look as considered as the kettle and the coffee cup sitting next to them. Objects you leave out, not hide under the sink.

We design from the small apartment outward: quiet pumps, footprints that fit a counter, materials that stay clean without effort. Every product is meant to be taken apart, washed, and kept for years, with the filter and pump replaceable when they wear. Good design here means less clutter, less noise, and one less thing to apologize for.

04 — What we care about

Quiet, honest, kept

Four ideas we measure every product against.

01

Quiet by design

A fountain should not hum over your evening. We aim for 24 decibels at one metre — soft enough that you forget it is running.

02

Easy to clean

Every part that touches water or food comes apart without tools, and all of it is dishwasher-safe. Five pieces, thirty seconds, and nothing left to soak.

03

Materials that last

We use 316 stainless, glazed stoneware and food-grade silicone, and keep the pump and filter replaceable so the object outlives the parts that wear out.

04

Honest about toys

Our toys are built for washing and for a determined chewer, but they are not indestructible. We would rather tell you that than pretend.
