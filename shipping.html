Shipping

Everything ships from one US warehouse, packed to survive the trip. US orders over $120 ship free; everywhere else, shipping is calculated at checkout.

US shipping

Free on orders over $120. Below that, a flat $8.

Orders leave the warehouse within two to four business days. You get a tracking link the moment the carrier picks it up.

EU and UK

Shipping is calculated at checkout, based on your address.

Duties and taxes are not included in our prices. They are collected on delivery, as your country requires.

Delivery times

US orders usually arrive within five to eight business days of leaving the warehouse.

EU and UK orders typically take seven to fourteen business days, depending on customs.

If something looks wrong

Check the box before you accept it. If it's damaged or items are missing, tell us within seven days and we'll sort it out.
